Our Story

A journey of thirty-five years, told one blessing at a time.

1990
A seed in Amreshwar

Bal Sanskar Kendra, Swadhyay Kendra, Deep Yagna, and the Jhola Pustakalay begin carrying Gurudev's message door to door, on foot and by bullock cart.

1998
Reaching Dabhoi

The Swadhyay Kendra takes root. Kanya Kaushalya Shibir begins — 35 camps and counting, shaping thousands of young girls.

1999
The Pragya Puran Katha begins

Rashmikaben Patel — Pragyaputri — starts telling stories that carry Gurudev's seven missions into every home that listens.

2003
Maa Bhagwati Pragya Bhavan

Established in Dabhoi with the blessings of Dr. Pranav Pandya, as the family outgrows its first home.

2006
Shri Ram Shraddha Bhavan

Inaugurated by Shukla Baba of Bihar — a larger home for a growing devotion.

2016
The Gurukul opens

24 tribal girls welcomed with free education, values, and a path to self-reliance — a promise kept for Param Vandaniya Mataji.

2017
Shivalaya consecrated

Its heart had already been beating since 2003 — daily Yagna, meditation, and evening aarti, without pause.

2022
Land given, in Bhorda

Rashmikaben donates her ancestral land. Gayatri Kamdhenu Sevatirth is born from that single act of giving.

2023
108 Kundi Mahayagya

A grand Mahayagya, presided over by Dr. Chinmay Pandya — not an ending, but a beginning for the Sevatirth.

TODAY
A living sanctuary

64+ tribal girls call it home. 30+ cows are cared for daily. Elders find dignity here. Every prayer, still building.
